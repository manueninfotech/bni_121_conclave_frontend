# Privacy Policy — BNI 121 Conclave

**Last updated: 6 September 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Manuen Infotech** ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the **BNI 121 Conclave** mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

If you have any questions, contact us at **connect@manuen.com**.

---

## 1. Who this App is for

BNI 121 Conclave is a networking app for members and guests attending BNI 121 Conclave events. It helps you register for conclaves, find and connect with other members, request one‑to‑one meetings, pass and track referrals, and receive event updates.

The App is intended for business professionals aged 18 and over. It is **not** directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

---

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
When you create an account and build your profile, we collect:

- **Contact details** — your phone number and email address (we ask for both so event organisers can reach you).
- **Identity & business details** — your name, business name, business category, location/city, and optionally your BNI chapter and region.
- **Membership type** — whether you are a BNI or Non‑BNI member.
- **Networking preferences** — what business you are looking for and what you can offer.
- **Profile photo** — if you choose to add one.
- **Content you create in the App** — referrals you pass or receive (including "thank you for closed business" amounts you enter), one‑to‑one meeting requests, and attendance during conclave rounds.

### 2.2 Information collected automatically
- **Authentication data** — managed by Google Firebase Authentication to sign you in securely (via a one‑time SMS code and/or email and password).
- **Device notification token** — so we can send you relevant push notifications (e.g. a round starting, a referral received, a one‑to‑one request).
- **Basic technical/diagnostic data** — such as app version and error information, used to keep the App reliable.

### 2.3 Device permissions we request
We only request a permission when a feature needs it, and we explain why at the moment we ask:

- **Camera** — to scan member QR codes for attendance and to take a profile photo.
- **Photo Library** — to choose a profile photo.
- **Contacts** — only when you choose to save a member you met to your phone's contacts. We do **not** read, upload, or store your contact list.
- **Notifications** — to deliver the event and networking alerts described above.

You can change or revoke any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.

---

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account and profile.
- Show you in the member directory (only safe, non‑contact fields — see Section 4) and power member matchmaking.
- Let you request and manage one‑to‑one meetings and pass, receive, and track referrals.
- Generate conclave seating and run event rounds.
- Send you notifications relevant to your conclave and your networking activity.
- Allow event organisers to contact you about the conclave.
- Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the App.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** use it for third‑party advertising.

---

## 4. What other members can see

Other members of the App can see a limited, safe subset of your profile in the member directory — such as your **name, business name, business category, location, membership type, chapter/region, profile photo, and your "looking for / can offer" preferences**.

Your **direct contact details (phone number and email) are not shown in the public directory.** They are shared only with event organisers so they can reach you about the conclave.

---

## 5. How your information is stored and shared

Your information is stored and processed using trusted service providers acting on our behalf:

- **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Storage for photos, and Cloud Messaging for notifications) — Google LLC.
- **Application backend hosting** — our server infrastructure hosted with Render.
- **Payments** — if your conclave involves a paid registration, payment is processed by our payment provider (**Razorpay**). We do not store your full card or banking details; those are handled directly by the payment provider under their own privacy terms.

These providers process your data only to provide their service to us and are bound by their own privacy and security obligations. Data may be processed on servers located outside your country; where that happens, we rely on the providers' safeguards for such transfers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the public.

---

## 6. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App and to comply with our legal obligations. When you delete your account, we delete or de‑identify your personal profile data, except where we are required to retain certain records (for example, transaction records) by law.

---

## 7. Your rights and choices

You can:

- **Access and update** your profile information at any time from within the App.
- **Control permissions** (camera, photos, contacts, notifications) in your device settings.
- **Request deletion** of your account and associated personal data by contacting us at **connect@manuen.com**. We will action verified requests within a reasonable period.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable data‑protection laws (such as the right to access, correct, or erase your data). Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

---

## 8. Security

We use industry‑standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and the access controls provided by our infrastructure providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and to promptly address any issues we become aware of.

---

## 9. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults (18+) in a professional networking context. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
