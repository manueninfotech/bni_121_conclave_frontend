BNI 121 Conclave is the companion app for members and guests at BNI 121 Conclave networking events.

• Register for conclaves and check in fast with QR codes
• Browse the member directory and find the right people to meet
• Request and manage one-to-one meetings
• Pass referrals, track their outcome, and record closed business
• Get timely alerts as rounds begin and referrals arrive
• Save the people you meet straight to your contacts

Built for the room, tuned for the day — everything you need to make your conclave count.
