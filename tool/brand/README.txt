1-2-1 CONNECT BRAND KIT

Everything the marketing team needs to present 1-2-1 Connect. The full guide
(logo use, colours, type, voice) is the brand guidelines page shared with this kit.

01-logo
  svg/  Vector files. Use these for print and anything that gets resized.
  png/  The same, as large transparent PNGs, plus previews on red, paper and ink.
  mark-*              The symbol alone (red, ink, white).
  wordmark-*          "1-2-1 Connect" alone.
  lockup-horizontal-* Symbol + name side by side. The default logo.
  lockup-stacked-*    Symbol above the name, for square spaces.
  -colour = red symbol, ink name (light backgrounds)
  -white  = all white (red, dark or photo backgrounds)
  -ink    = all ink (one-colour print, stamps, light backgrounds)

02-app-icon     The App Store / Play icon (1024, 512, 180) and a rounded preview for slides.
03-colour       palette.png and colours.txt (HEX, RGB, CMYK).
04-type         Plus Jakarta Sans, free under the Open Font License.
05-screens      Real app screens (demo data, fictional people) and the store screenshots.
06-social       Ready-to-post images: link preview, Instagram posts and story, LinkedIn banner.
07-copy         copy.md: the name, taglines, descriptions, boilerplate, links, voice.
