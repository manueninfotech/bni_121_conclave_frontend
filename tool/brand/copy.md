# 1-2-1 Connect: copy deck

Ready-to-use words for posts, ads, decks and listings. Use them as written, or keep to the same facts and tone.

## The name

- Write it **1-2-1 Connect**: digits with hyphens, a space, capital C.
- Not: 121 Connect, 1-2-1connect, One-to-One Connect, 1:1 Connect, 1-2-1 CONNECT.
- On its own, "1-2-1" means the meeting format (a one-to-one), not the app.
- The company behind it: **Manuen Infotech**.

- Your conclave, round by round.

## Descriptions

**One line (up to 80 characters)**
Structured 1-2-1 networking for BNI conclaves: your table, referrals, contacts.

**Short (about 30 words)**
1-2-1 Connect runs your BNI 1-2-1 conclave from your phone: where to sit, who is speaking, when to pass business, and everyone you met, saved when the day is done.

**Long (about 80 words)**
1-2-1 Connect is the companion app for BNI 1-2-1 conclaves. It seats you at a balanced table each round, shows who is speaking with a live timer every phone at the table shares, and opens the referral slot at the right moment so you can pass business in one tap. Attendance works even without signal. After the conclave, your summary shows every round you attended, the referrals you gave and received, and everyone you met, ready to save to your contacts.

## Boilerplate (for press and partner pages)

1-2-1 Connect is a networking app for BNI 1-2-1 conclaves, built by Manuen Infotech in Guntur, Andhra Pradesh. It handles seating, live rounds, referrals and follow-ups for members, guests and organisers, on iPhone and Android. Learn more at manuen.com/conclave.

## Key messages

1. **Know where to be.** Your table for every round, and where to go next.
2. **Know who is speaking.** A live timer every phone at the table shares.
3. **Pass business in one tap.** Referrals open in the referral slot, right at the table.
4. **Works without signal.** Attendance and referrals sync when you are back online.
5. **Keep everyone you met.** A summary of your day, ready to save to your contacts.
6. **Fair tables.** Balanced sizes, no two people from the same business category, and organisers can keep chapter members apart.

## Voice

- Plain and specific. Say what the app does in the room ("Your table for every round"), not abstract claims ("revolutionising networking").
- Short sentences, active voice.
- No emojis in brand copy, captions or the app.
- Numbers as digits: 60 seconds, 6 rounds, 3 to 8 people a table.
