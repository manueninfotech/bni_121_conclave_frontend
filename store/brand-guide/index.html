Every table. Every round. Every referral.

The brand guide for 1-2-1 Connect, the networking app for BNI 1-2-1 conclaves by Manuen Infotech. Use it with the brand kit files when you make posts, decks, flyers and banners.

Name

Write it 1-2-1 Connect

Use

  • 1-2-1 Connect: digits with hyphens, a space, capital C
  • "1-2-1" on its own means the meeting, a one-to-one
  • "by Manuen Infotech" when you name the maker

Avoid

  • 121 Connect, 1-2-1connect, 1:1 Connect, One-to-One Connect
  • The old name, BNI 1-2-1 Conclave, and its logo
  • Calling it an official BNI product
Logo

Three versions, one for each background

The symbol is two people meeting across a table. Use the horizontal logo by default and the stacked one in square spaces. Use the vector SVG files for print and anything that gets resized.

ColourRed symbol, ink name. White and light backgrounds.
WhiteRed, dark and photo backgrounds.
InkOne-colour print, stamps, embossing.
StackedSquare spaces: profile pictures, stickers, merchandise.
Symbol aloneOnly where the name already appears nearby, or as an app-like icon.
App iconFor "download the app" moments. Keep it a rounded square; never redraw it.

Clear space and size

  • Keep empty space around the logo at least the height of one head in the symbol (the dashed line).
  • Smallest horizontal logo: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print.
  • Smallest symbol alone: 24 px, 8 mm in print.

Don't

  • Recolour it beyond the three versions, or add gradients, outlines or shadows.
  • Stretch, rotate, or rearrange the symbol and name.
  • Retype the name in another font, or put the colour version on red or busy photos.
Colour

Red leads, warm neutrals support

Use red with intent: the logo, a hero background, or the one action you want people to take. Most layouts are paper and ink with red as the accent, about 60% neutral, 30% red and 10% the rest.

Type

Plus Jakarta Sans, everywhere

The same face as the app and the website. It's free under the Open Font License (in the kit, and on Google Fonts). Headlines are ExtraBold with slightly tight spacing; body text is Medium or Regular.

Voice

Plain, specific, about the room

Say what the app does at a conclave, in short sentences. Numbers as digits. No emojis in brand copy, captions or the app.

Like this

"Your table for every round, and where to go next."

"Pass business in one tap, right at the table."

Not like this

"Revolutionising the future of networking!"

"Seamless, AI-powered synergy for your network."

Imagery

Show the real app

Use the app screens from the kit: they're the real app with demo data and fictional people. Never put real members' names, numbers or photos in marketing without their permission.

Links

Where to send people

For download buttons, use Apple's and Google's official badges unchanged (developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines and play.google.com/intl/en_us/badges).
