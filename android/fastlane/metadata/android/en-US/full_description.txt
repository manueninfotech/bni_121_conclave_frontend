1-2-1 Connect is the companion app for BNI 1-2-1 conclaves: structured one-to-one networking, from your first table to your last referral.

YOUR SEAT, EVERY ROUND
- See your table, the people at it and the speaking order
- A live timer shows who is speaking and how long they have left
- Read each person's ask before they speak
- When the round ends, the app tells you where to go next

PASS BUSINESS IN ONE TAP
- Give a referral to anyone at your table during the referral slot
- Add a note so the lead is clear
- Track every referral you give and receive, and record closed business

ATTENDANCE THAT WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
- Table captains scan you in with your QR code
- Works offline at the venue and syncs when you are back online

SEE WHAT YOU BUILT
- After the conclave, your summary shows your rounds, referrals and everyone you met
- Save the people you met straight to your contacts
- Share your own contact card with a QR code

ALSO
- Browse upcoming conclaves and register in seconds
- Set your Conclave Specific Ask so your table knows what you need
- Request and manage 1-2-1 meetings with other members
- Get alerts as rounds begin and referrals arrive

1-2-1 Connect requires an account. Any payment in the app is a registration fee for a physical networking event.
