Signing in

How do I sign in?

Open the app, choose Phone or Email, and enter the number or email you registered with and your password. New here? Tap "Create an account" and follow the steps.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?". With a phone account we text you a 6-digit code, then you choose a new password. With an email account we send a reset link to your inbox; open it on the same phone.

I did not get the SMS code

Check the number and country code, make sure your phone has signal, and wait a minute before asking for a new code. If it still does not arrive, email us and we will help.

Conclaves

How do I register for a conclave?

Go to the Conclaves tab, open an upcoming conclave and tap Register. Check that your business category is right before you confirm: it decides who you share a table with.

Can I cancel my registration?

Not in the app. The schedule seats and pairs you with other members, so an empty seat affects their tables. If you really cannot attend, contact the conclave's organiser as early as you can.

I paid but I am not shown as registered

Close and reopen the app, then check the conclave again. If you are still not registered, email us with your registered phone number and the payment reference from Razorpay, and we will sort it out.

What is the Conclave Specific Ask?

One line about the business you are looking for at that conclave. The people at your table see it when it is your turn, so they can think of referrals for you.

On the day

How is my attendance recorded?

At conclaves with table captains, the captain scans your QR code each round. At conclaves without captains, you tap "I'm here". Both work without network and sync later.

When can I give a referral?

During the referral slot of each round, after everyone at the table has given their bio. The app opens the "Give a referral" button at the right time.

My summary shows fewer referrals than I expected

Some records live on other people's phones until they sync. Connect to the internet and tap the refresh button on your conclave summary.

I am not getting notifications

Make sure notifications are allowed for 1-2-1 Connect in your phone's settings, and that the app is updated to the latest version.

Your account

How do I change my details?

Go to Profile, then Edit profile. Changes apply to conclaves scheduled from then on; a conclave already under way keeps the details it started with.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile, then Delete account, and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and profile, your networking details, referrals and one-to-ones. You can also email us from your registered email and we will delete it for you.

How is my data used?

See our Privacy Policy. Other members never see your phone number or email in the directory.
