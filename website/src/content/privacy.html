This Privacy Policy explains how Manuen Infotech ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the 1-2-1 Connect mobile app (the "App"), formerly called BNI 1-2-1 Conclave. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

If you have any questions, contact us at connect@manuen.com.

1. Who the App is for

1-2-1 Connect is a networking app for members and guests attending BNI 1-2-1 conclaves. It helps you register for conclaves, follow your table and rounds on the day, record attendance, pass and track referrals, request one-to-one meetings and receive event updates.

The App is meant for business professionals aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details: your phone number and email address.
  • Identity and business details: your name, business name, business category, location, and optionally your BNI chapter and region.
  • Membership type: whether you are a BNI or Non-BNI member.
  • Networking details: what you are looking for, what you can offer, and your Conclave Specific Ask.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to add one.
  • What you create in the App: conclave registrations, attendance in each round, referrals you give or receive (including notes and any closed-business amounts you enter), and one-to-one meeting requests.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in data: handled by Google Firebase Authentication, using a one-time SMS code and/or your email and password.
  • Notification token: so we can send you alerts such as a round starting or a referral received.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: app version, device type, which screens are used, crash reports and performance measurements. We use this to fix problems and improve the App.

2.3 Device permissions

We ask for a permission only when a feature needs it:

  • Camera: for table captains to scan members' QR codes for attendance, and to take a profile photo.
  • Photos: to choose a profile photo.
  • Contacts: only when you choose to save someone you met. We do not read, upload or store your contact list.
  • Calendar: only to add a conclave you registered for. We do not read your calendar.
  • Notifications: to deliver event and networking alerts.

You can change any of these permissions at any time in your device settings.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and profile.
  • To seat you at conclaves (your business category decides who you share a table with) and to run the rounds.
  • To record attendance and let you give, receive and track referrals and one-to-one meetings.
  • To show you in the member directory (see Section 4).
  • To send you notifications about your conclaves and networking activity.
  • To let conclave organisers contact you about the event.
  • To process registration fees for paid conclaves.
  • To keep the App secure and reliable, and to understand how it is used so we can improve it.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. What other people can see

Other members can see a limited part of your profile: your name, business name, business category, location, membership type, chapter and region, profile photo, and your networking details. The people at your table during a conclave also see your Specific Ask.

Your phone number and email are not shown in the member directory. They are shared with conclave organisers so they can reach you. After a conclave, people you shared a table with can save your contact details to their phone from their summary, as part of the networking the event is for.

5. Where your information is stored and who processes it

We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Microsoft Azure (Central India region): our application servers, database and profile photo storage.
  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): sign-in, push notifications, app analytics, crash reporting, performance monitoring, remote configuration and app integrity checks.
  • Razorpay: payments for paid conclave registrations. We do not see or store your full card or bank details; Razorpay handles them under its own privacy terms.

Some of these providers may process data outside your country. Where that happens, we rely on their safeguards for such transfers. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the public.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and as needed to provide the App. When you delete your account, we delete your profile and the data linked to it, except records we must keep by law (for example, payment records).

7. Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile at any time in the App (Profile, then Edit profile).
  • Delete your account in the App (Profile, then Delete account). This removes your account and profile, your networking details, referrals and one-to-ones. You can also email connect@manuen.com from your registered email to ask us to do it.
  • Control permissions in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have further rights, such as to access, correct or erase your data, or to nominate someone to act for you. Contact us to use any of them.

8. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and the access controls of our infrastructure providers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and act quickly on any issue we learn of.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the App. Using the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
