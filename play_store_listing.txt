LONG DESCRIPTION  (Play limit: 4000 characters)
------------------------------------------------------------
BNI 121 Conclave brings structure to in-person referral networking. Built for members of networking chapters, it runs your 1-to-1 conclave from start to finish — seating, rounds, attendance, and referrals — so every meeting counts.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Register in seconds
Sign up with your phone number or email, add your business details, and register for upcoming conclaves in a few taps.

Know your seat, every round
See exactly which table you are on and who you are meeting, round by round. Your schedule updates the moment a new round begins, so you are never guessing where to go.

Fair, category-based seating
Seating follows one clear rule: no two people from the same business category ever share a table. You meet new professionals from different fields in every round.

Attendance that works offline
Event venues rarely have reliable signal. Table captains scan members' QR codes to record attendance, and it works with no connection at all — everything syncs automatically the moment you are back online. No lost records, no manual roll-calls.

Referrals in one tap
Pass business to the people at your table with a single tap. Add a note if you want to, or skip it. Your referrals are recorded and ready to follow up on later.

Round alerts
Get a notification the moment a new round starts, so you are always on time and at the right table.

Your conclave summary
After the event, review everything you did — the connections you made and the referrals you gave and received — all in one place.

BUILT FOR BUSINESS PROFESSIONALS

The app is clean, quick, and straightforward whether you are a first-time member or a seasoned networker. It adapts to your chosen text size and lays out comfortably on phones of every screen size, so it stays readable and easy to use in a busy room.

SECURE BY DESIGN

Your account is protected with secure sign-in, and your information is handled with server-side access controls. The camera is used only for captains to scan attendance QR codes — no photos or video are ever captured or stored.

Read our privacy policy at: manuen.com/conclave/privacy

HOW IT WORKS

1. Register for an upcoming conclave and complete your profile.
2. When the event begins, open the app to see your table and the members in your round.
3. Captains scan members in to record attendance for each round.
4. Give referrals to the people you meet with a single tap.
5. Move to your next table when the next round starts.
6. Review your full summary once the conclave ends.
